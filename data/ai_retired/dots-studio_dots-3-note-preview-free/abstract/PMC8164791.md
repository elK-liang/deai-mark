This study investigated the association between ADH1B polymorphism, alcohol consumption, and the risk of intracerebral hemorrhagic stroke. Data were linked from the Taiwan Biobank (TWB) and the National Health Insurance Research Database (NHIRD), yielding reliable data for 890 individuals with ischemic stroke, 70 with hemorrhagic stroke, and 16,837 controls. Participants with ischemic stroke were older and had a lower education status, and both stroke groups showed a higher prevalence of cerebrovascular risk factors compared to controls. While the ADH1B rs1229984 variant was not associated with ischemic stroke based on alcohol intake, significant associations were observed with diabetes, hypertension, and hyperlipidemia among individuals with the TT and TC genotypes. These findings suggest that the ADH1B rs1229984 variant may influence stroke risk through its relationship with metabolic comorbidities rather than directly via alcohol consumption in ischemic stroke.
