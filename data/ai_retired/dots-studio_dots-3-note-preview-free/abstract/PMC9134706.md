In esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC), the identification of key oncogenic long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs) provides new avenues for understanding tumor progression. Here, we screened differentially expressed lncRNAs in two independent ESCC cohorts and identified DLEU1 as a highly overexpressed lncRNA in tumor tissues compared to matched adjacent normal controls, a finding validated in The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) database and by RNAscope assays. Clinically, elevated DLEU1 expression was significantly correlated with lymph node metastasis, advanced clinical stages, and poor overall survival. Functional studies demonstrated that knockdown of DLEU1 markedly suppressed the growth of ESCC cells. Gene set enrichment analysis (GSEA) further showed that high DLEU1 expression is associated with gene sets regulating mitochondrial membrane permeability and the apoptotic process. Mechanistically, DLEU1 promotes cell survival by preventing DYNLL1 degradation, thereby inhibiting apoptosis and contributing to the aggressive phenotype of ESCC. These findings highlight DLEU1 as a critical regulator of ESCC progression and a potential prognostic biomarker and therapeutic target.
