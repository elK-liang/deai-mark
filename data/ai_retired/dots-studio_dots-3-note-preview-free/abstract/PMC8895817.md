Targeted panel sequencing in 77 healthy adolescents from Southern Italy identified 1964 variants, of which 208 were located in the LPL, FNDC5 and PPARγ genes; after quality filtering using FAO, FDP, GQ and QUAL, 111 SNPs (61 in LPL, 38 in FNDC5, 12 in PPARγ) remained, consisting of 33 indels, 77 single‑nucleotide variants and one substitution. The substitution types were A/G 14.1%, A/C 7.5%, A/T 5%, C/A 8.7%, C/T 16.6%, C/G 5.1%, G/A 14.1%, G/C 2.6%, G/T 6.4%, T/A 2.6%, T/C 16.6%, T/G 2.6%. Twenty‑four variants were novel, with 11 in LPL, 12 in FNDC5 and one in PPARγ, including 21 SNVs and 3 indels. Among the 111 variants, 40 were intronic, 53 in flanking regions (42 in 3′UTR, 4 in 5′UTR, 4 upstream, 1 intergenic, 2 regulatory) and 18 exonic (4 frameshift, 14 single substitutions, one stop codon). The exonic variants included three non‑synonymous changes in PPARγ (P12A, G290W, K156E) and two frameshifts (L178Tfs2, D7Rfs5), three in FNDC5 (L132Ffs6, L81V, H127N), and nine in LPL (four synonymous, four non‑synonymous, one stop codon S474X, one frameshift Q118Hfs30). Five novel variants had MAF ≥0.05, two had MAF 0.05–0.01, and 17 had MAF <0.01. Using Haploview v4.2 with an r² threshold of 0.8, 52 common SNPs were identified as tag SNPs, comprising 34 in LPL, 19 in FNDC5 and 3 in PPARγ, with no linkage disequilibrium detected for FNDC5 and PPARγ.
