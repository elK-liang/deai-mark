This study characterizes LPL, FNDC5, and PPARγ gene polymorphisms related to body composition parameters and lipid metabolic profiles in 77 healthy adolescents from Southern Italy. Targeted panel sequencing identified 1964 total variants, of which 208 were located in the genes of interest. After filtering for quality parameters, 111 single nucleotide polymorphisms were confirmed as true positive calls, comprising 61 in LPL, 38 in FNDC5, and 12 in PPARγ. Twenty-four of these variants were novel, including 11 in LPL, 12 in FNDC5, and 1 in PPARγ, with the majority (17) exhibiting a minor allele frequency of less than 0.01. Genomic distribution of the 111 variants revealed 40 in intronic regions, 53 in flanking regions, and 18 exonic variants. The exonic variants included frameshifts, a stop codon variant in LPL (S474X), and several non-synonymous substitutions such as P12A in PPARγ, L81V in FNDC5, and N318S in LPL. Using Haploview software v4.2 with an R2 value of 0.8, 52 tagSNPs were identified, distributed as 34 in LPL, 19 in FNDC5, and 3 in PPARγ. Linkage disequilibrium analysis showed no SNPs in LD for FNDC5 and PPARγ. These findings provide a detailed catalog of genetic variants in key metabolic genes, including a significant number of novel mutations, which establishes a foundation for future association studies with anthropometric parameters and lipid profiles.
