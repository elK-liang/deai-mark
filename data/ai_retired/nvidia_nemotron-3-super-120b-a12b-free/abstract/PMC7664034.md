To examine the putative positive role of inflammatory genes in fat deposition, we compared gene expression profiles of purified adipocytes and preadipocytes derived from lean and obese human adipose tissue.

mRNA‑seq was performed on four sample groups: lean adipocytes (L‑AC), obese adipocytes (O‑AC), lean preadipocytes (L‑preAC), and obese preadipocytes (O‑preAC). Four DEG classes were defined:  
- Class I: L‑AC vs O‑AC  
- Class II: L‑preAC vs O‑preAC  
- Class III: L‑preAC vs L‑AC (lean adipogenesis)  
- Class IV: O‑preAC vs O‑AC (obese adipogenesis)  
DEGs were identified using various p‑value and false‑discovery‑rate thresholds (see Supplementary Table S1). Unsupervised hierarchical clustering and heat‑map visualization assessed sample separation. Validation was carried out by comparing our DEG lists with those reported in GSE80654 (microarray ACs from lean vs obese subjects).

Class I analysis yielded 1,198 genes significant at p < 0.01 and 314 genes at FDR < 0.05 (Supplementary Table S1). Hierarchical clustering of L‑AC and O‑AC samples using these DEGs did not produce clear separation, indicating considerable transcriptional overlap between lean and obese adipocytes. DEG sets were also obtained for Classes II‑IV. Class III (lean adipogenesis) and Class IV (obese adipogenesis) revealed distinct gene‑expression changes associated with differentiation, with enrichment of inflammatory‑signaling and lipid‑metabolism genes. Comparison with the GSE80654 dataset showed a moderate concordance of DEG signatures, supporting the reliability of our observations.

Although overall transcriptional differences between lean and obese adipocytes are modest, specific alterations in inflammatory and lipid‑related genes are evident during adipogenesis in both lean and obese contexts. These findings support a putative positive role of inflammatory pathways in fat deposition.
