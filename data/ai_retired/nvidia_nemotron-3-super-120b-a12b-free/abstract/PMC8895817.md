We conducted targeted panel sequencing of the LPL, FNDC5 and PPARγ genes in 77 healthy adolescents from Southern Italy, identifying a total of 1 964 variants, of which 208 mapped to the three genes of interest; after applying quality filters (FAO, FDP, GQ, QUAL) 111 high‑quality variants remained (61 in LPL, 38 in FNDC5 and 12 in PPARγ), comprising 33 insertions/deletions, 77 single‑nucleotide variants and one substitution. The spectrum of substitutions showed the following proportions: A/G 14.1 %; A/C 7.5 %; A/T 5.0 %; C/A 8.7 %; C/T 16.6 %; C/G 5.1 %; G/A 14.1 %; G/C 2.6 %; G/T 6.4 %; T/A 2.6 %; T/C 16.6 %; T/G 2.6 %. Twenty‑four variants were novel, with 11 in LPL, 12 in FNDC5 and 1 in PPARγ (21 SNVs and 3 indels). Among the 18 coding variants (4 frameshifts and 14 single substitutions, including one stop codon) we observed in PPARγ the non‑synonymous changes P12A, G290W, K156E and the frameshifts L178Tfs2 and D7Rfs5; in FNDC5 a frameshift L132Ffs6 and the SNVs L81V and H127N; and in LPL four synonymous changes (V135V, T388T, L81L, E145E), four non‑synonymous changes (N318S, R197H, H348P, E36N), a stop codon S474X and a frameshift Q118Hfs30. Of the novel variants, five had a minor allele frequency (MAF) ≥0.05, two showed MAF between 0.05 and 0.01, and seventeen were rare with MAF <0.01. Using Haploview v4.2 with an r² threshold of 0.8, we defined 52 common tagSNPs across the cohort (34 in LPL, 19 in FNDC5 and 3 in PPARγ); linkage disequilibrium analysis indicated limited LD for FNDC5 and PPARγ in this sample. These findings expand the catalogue of genetic variation in LPL, FNDC5 and PPARγ among Southern Italian adolescents and provide a foundation for future investigations of how these polymorphisms may influence body composition and lipid metabolism.
