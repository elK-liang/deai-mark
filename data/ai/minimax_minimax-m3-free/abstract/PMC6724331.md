Abstract

Circular RNAs (circRNAs) are a class of endogenous noncoding RNAs that have recently been recognized as important regulators of gene expression. However, the expression landscape and biological roles of circRNAs in non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), particularly in its two major histological subtypes, lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) and lung squamous cell carcinoma (LUSC), remain largely unexplored.

Ribosomal RNA-depleted total RNAs from 10 pairs of NSCLC tumors and their corresponding adjacent normal tissues were treated with RNase R to degrade linear RNAs and enrich circRNAs. High-throughput RNA sequencing was then performed, and five independent computational programs (CIRCexplorer2, circRNA_finder, CIRI2, find_circ, and MapSplice) were employed in parallel to comprehensively screen reliable circRNAs. Differentially expressed circRNAs between tumor and matched normal tissues were identified using stringent criteria (corrected p value ≤ 0.05 and fold change ≥ 2). Selected candidates were further validated by Sanger sequencing and quantitative PCR.

A total of 17,952 circRNAs were consistently identified across all five programs, with approximately 98.84% supported by at least two unique back-spliced reads. These circRNAs were distributed across all genomic regions, although most were derived from exonic regions, with 77.4% consisting of 2–5 exons and 91.7% ranging from 200 to 1200 nucleotides in length. Of the identified circRNAs, 15,359 were annotated in circBase, while 2,593 represented novel circRNAs. Notably, ~60.8% of host genes produced at least two distinct circRNAs through alternative back-splicing, with some genes generating more than 10 circRNA isoforms. Comparative analysis revealed 50 circRNAs differentially expressed in LUAD tissues relative to their normal counterparts. The upregulation of hsa_circ_0002360 in LUAD was successfully validated by Sanger sequencing and qPCR.

Our study provides a comprehensive circRNA expression profile in NSCLC and identifies differentially expressed circRNAs, including hsa_circ_0002360, that may serve as candidate biomarkers or therapeutic targets for lung adenocarcinoma.
