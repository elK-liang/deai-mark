Genetic variations in genes regulating lipid metabolism and adipocyte differentiation, such as LPL, FNDC5, and PPARγ, may contribute to inter-individual variability in body composition and lipid profiles, particularly during adolescence when these parameters undergo significant physiological changes. The present study employed targeted panel sequencing in 77 healthy adolescents from Southern Italy to investigate the genetic variability of these three candidate genes and to explore their potential associations with anthropometric and lipid metabolic traits. A total of 1964 variants were initially identified, of which 208 mapped to the three genes of interest, and 111 passed stringent quality filtering (61 in LPL, 38 in FNDC5, and 12 in PPARγ), comprising 77 single nucleotide variants, 33 insertions/deletions, and 1 substitution. Twenty-four of these variants had not been previously described in the literature, including 21 single nucleotide variants and 3 small indels, with 5 showing minor allele frequencies ≥0.05, 2 between 0.01 and 0.05, and 17 below 0.01. The majority of the detected variants mapped to intronic (n=40) and flanking regions (n=53), whereas 18 were exonic, including 14 non-synonymous single nucleotide variants, 4 frameshifts, and 1 stop codon variant, with notable coding changes identified in PPARγ (P12A, G290W, K156E, L178Tfs2, D7Rfs5), FNDC5 (L81V, H127N, L132Ffs6), and LPL (N318S, R197H, H348P, E36N, S474X, Q118Hfs30). Linkage disequilibrium analysis using Haploview v4.2 at an r² threshold of 0.8 identified 52 tag single nucleotide polymorphisms (34 in LPL, 19 in FNDC5, and 3 in PPARγ), allowing a reduction in the number of variants to be tested in downstream analyses while preserving coverage of the common genetic variation within these loci. These findings expand the catalogue of known variation in LPL, FNDC5, and PPARγ in a Mediterranean adolescent population and provide a foundation for further analyses aimed at correlating these polymorphisms with body composition parameters and lipid metabolic traits.
