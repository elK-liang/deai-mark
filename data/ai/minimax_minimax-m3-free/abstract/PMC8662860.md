Non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) is a heterogeneous disease with complex immune landscapes that contribute to variable clinical outcomes. Elucidating immune-based subtypes of NSCLC may help refine prognostic stratification and guide immunotherapy strategies.

We performed a multi-omics analysis integrating transcriptomic data from NSCLC patients using the LM22 immune signature matrix. Non-negative matrix factorization (NMF) clustering was applied to define immune subtypes, and principal component analysis (PCA) was used to assess the separation among subgroups. Monocle trajectory analysis was conducted to infer the pseudotime ordering of immune signature activity across subtypes. The proportions of 22 immune cell populations were compared among the identified NMF subgroups.

NMF clustering identified three subgroups (NMF1, NMF2, NMF3) with distinct spatial distributions on the PCA plot. PC2 efficiently separated NSCLC cases into lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) and lung squamous cell carcinoma (LUSC), largely coinciding with NMF grouping: NMF1 was predominantly LUSC (92.24%, 333/361), NMF3 was predominantly LUAD (96.23%, 357/371), and NMF2 contained roughly equal proportions of LUAD (45.39%, 128/282) and LUSC (54.61%, 154/282). Accordingly, the NMF2 sub-cluster was subdivided into NMF2A (LUSC) and NMF2B (LUAD), yielding four subgroups overall. Monocle pseudotime analysis revealed that NMF2A and NMF2B groups occupied the developing end of the immune spectrum, indicating highly active immune components, whereas the NMF1 group exhibited the least active immune components. The NMF3 subgroup showed internal heterogeneity, although intragroup differences were much smaller than intergroup ones. Comparative analysis of immune cell composition demonstrated distinct cellular profiles among subgroups: the NMF2 group was enriched for CD8 T cells, activated CD4 memory T cells, and M1 macrophages; the NMF1 group was characterized by higher proportions of resting NK cells, M0 macrophages, and activated mast cells; and the NMF3 group showed higher proportions of resting memory CD4 T cells, M2 macrophages, and resting dendritic cells.

Integration of NMF clustering, PCA, and pseudotime trajectory analysis defined four immune-based subgroups of NSCLC with distinct cellular compositions and immune activation states. These subgroups provide an immune-based framework for prognostic prediction and may inform personalized immunotherapy approaches in NSCLC.
