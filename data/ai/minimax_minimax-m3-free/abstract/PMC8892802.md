Abstract

Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) is a heterogeneous disease with recognized molecular subtypes that carry prognostic significance. The classification framework described by Chapuy et al. identified distinct genetic clusters in de novo DLBCL using whole-genome and whole-exome approaches. Whether targeted next-generation sequencing panels can reproduce these molecular subgroups and their prognostic implications in de novo DLBCL remains an open question.

We applied non-negative matrix factorization (NMF) clustering to de novo DLBCL patient samples profiled with the F1H targeted sequencing assay across three independent cohorts: the GOYA study (n = 397, biomarker-evaluable population), the CAVALLI study (n = 86), and the Foundation Medicine Flatiron Health-Foundation Medicine Clinico-Genomic Database (FH-FMI CGDB; n = 53). Identified clusters were compared with the genetic subgroups originally defined by Chapuy et al. and evaluated for concordance with cell-of-origin (COO) subtypes.

Baseline disease characteristics were similar between the biomarker-evaluable and intention-to-treat populations across studies. In GOYA, four clusters were identified: a BCL2/EZH2 Chapuy C3-like cluster (n = 141), a MYD88/CD79B C5-like cluster (n = 130), a NOTCH2/TNFAIP3 C1-like cluster (n = 126), and a 'no mutation' group (n = 26). In CAVALLI, three clusters were identified (MYD88/CD79B, n = 40; BCL2/EZH2, n = 40; 'no mutations', n = 6); a NOTCH2/TNFAIP3-like cluster was not observed. In the FH-FMI CGDB, three clusters were identified (BCL2/EZH2, n = 25; BCL2 Amplification/CDKN2A/B, n = 9; CD70/SGK1, n = 16), along with a 'no mutations' group (n = 3). The identified subgroups shared broadly similar mutational profiles with Chapuy et al.'s clusters, with BCL2/EZH2 mapping to C3, MYD88/CD79B to C5, and NOTCH2/TNFAIP3 to C1. However, Chapuy's C2 (TP53/copy-number alteration) could not be recapitulated due to the absence of genome-wide copy-number alterations in the F1H panel, with TP53 mutants clustering alongside BCL2/EZH2. Additionally, Chapuy's C4 (PI3K/NFKB) was not observed despite sufficient variant coverage in the F1H panel. The distribution of COO subtypes within identified clusters correlated well with those reported by Chapuy et al., with a notably high proportion of activated B-cell (ABC)-DLBCL tumors within the MYD88/CD79B C5-like cluster (n = 87/130, 67% in GOYA).

The F1H targeted sequencing assay can identify molecular subgroups in de novo DLBCL that are largely concordant with the genetic clusters originally defined by Chapuy et al., particularly BCL2/EZH2 (C3), MYD88/CD79B (C5), and NOTCH2/TNFAIP3 (C1) clusters. Limitations of targeted panels include the inability to recapitulate copy-number-driven subtypes such as Chapuy's C2, and the inconsistent detection of the C4 (PI3K/NFKB) cluster. These findings support the feasibility of mutational subtyping using targeted panels for prognostic stratification in de novo DLBCL, while highlighting subtype-specific considerations for clinical implementation.
