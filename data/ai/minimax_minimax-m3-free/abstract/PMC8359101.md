Abstract

Circular RNAs (circRNAs) are increasingly recognized as important regulators in cancer biology; however, their roles and mechanisms in gastric cancer (GC) remain poorly understood. This study aimed to identify and characterize novel circRNAs involved in GC progression.

CircRNA microarray datasets (GSE83521 and GSE89143) from the Gene Expression Omnibus were analyzed to identify differentially expressed circRNAs in GC. The candidate circRNA hsa_circ_0061137 (circDIDO1) was validated by divergent primer PCR, Sanger sequencing, RNase R resistance assays, and RNA-FISH. Expression of circDIDO1 was examined in GC cell lines and 102 paired tumor/normal tissues from GC patients by qRT-PCR, and its clinical relevance was assessed.

CircDIDO1, formed by back-splicing of exons 2–6 of the DIDO1 gene (1787 nucleotides), was resistant to RNase R degradation and localized in both the nucleus and cytoplasm of GC cells. CircDIDO1 was significantly downregulated in GC cells and in tumor tissues compared with adjacent normal tissues. Low circDIDO1 expression was inversely correlated with tumor size and distant metastasis, and GC patients with low circDIDO1 expression had significantly shorter overall survival than those with high expression.

CircDIDO1 is a newly identified, downregulated circRNA in GC whose reduced expression is associated with aggressive clinicopathological features and poor prognosis, suggesting that circDIDO1 may serve as a prognostic biomarker and a potential therapeutic target in GC.
