Abstract

Circular RNAs (circRNAs) are a class of endogenous non-coding RNAs that have been increasingly recognized as important regulators in various human cancers. However, the expression patterns and functional roles of circRNAs in colon cancer (CC) remain largely undefined.

CircRNA microarray analysis was performed on paired colon cancer tissues and adjacent non-tumor tissues from 10 patients to identify differentially expressed circRNAs. The expression of circPPP1R12A (hsa_circ_0000423) was validated by quantitative real-time PCR in an independent cohort of 20 patients and in a panel of colon cancer cell lines. The circular nature of circPPP1R12A was confirmed using divergent and convergent primers, as well as an RNase R degradation assay.

A total of 126 circRNAs were differentially expressed between colon cancer tissues and matched adjacent non-tumor tissues (P < 0.05 and fold change > 1.5), of which 110 were up-regulated and 16 were down-regulated. These dysregulated circRNAs were predominantly located at exonic regions. Among them, circPPP1R12A was identified as the most up-regulated circRNA and was back-spliced from exons 24/25 of the PPP1R12A gene located at chromosome 12q21.2. The elevated expression of circPPP1R12A in colon cancer tissues was confirmed in an expanded cohort, whereas the linear PPP1R12A transcript showed comparable expression between tumor and matched control tissues. In addition, circPPP1R12A was consistently up-regulated in multiple colon cancer cell lines (HT-29, HCT-116, SW480, SW620, LoVo, SW48, DLD-1, Caco2 and HCT-15) compared with the normal human colon mucosal epithelial cell line NCM460, with the highest expression observed in HCT-116 cells, followed by LoVo cells. The circular form of circPPP1R12A was successfully amplified from both cDNA and gDNA by convergent primers, but only from cDNA by divergent primers, and exhibited resistance to RNase R digestion, confirming its circular structure.

Our findings reveal a distinct circRNA expression profile in colon cancer and identify circPPP1R12A as a significantly up-regulated circular RNA that may serve as a promising candidate for further functional investigation in colon cancer progression.
