Colorectal cancer (CRC) remains a leading cause of cancer-related mortality worldwide, and circular RNAs (circRNAs) have emerged as important regulators of tumor progression, although their functions and molecular mechanisms in CRC are not fully understood. In this study, we performed ribosomal RNA-depleted total RNA sequencing of six paired CRC and adjacent normal tissues and identified 71 differentially expressed circRNAs, including 32 upregulated and 39 downregulated circRNAs (log2 fold change > 1 or < −1; P < 0.05). Among these candidates, the upregulated circRNA hsa_circ_0006357, derived from exon 2 and exon 3 of the EZH2 gene and designated circEZH2, attracted our attention and was validated by polymerase chain reaction amplification and Sanger sequencing. Analysis of circEZH2 expression across six human CRC cell lines (COLO205, HCT15, LoVo, SW620, HT-29, and HCT116) and normal counterparts further supported its relevance to CRC. Together, these findings establish a comprehensive expression profile of circRNAs in CRC and identify circEZH2 as a promising candidate circRNA associated with colorectal cancer progression, providing the foundation for subsequent functional and mechanistic investigations of the circEZH2/miR-133b/IGF2BP2/CREB1 axis in CRC.
