Abstract

Acute graft-versus-host disease (aGVHD) remains a major complication of allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation. The balance between T helper 17 (Th17) cells and regulatory T (Treg) cells plays a critical role in aGVHD pathogenesis, and therapeutic strategies that restore this balance are needed. FK506, a calcineurin inhibitor widely used as a prophylactic immunosuppressant, and Lactobacillus acidophilus, a probiotic with immunomodulatory properties, may act synergistically to modulate T-cell differentiation.

We examined the effects of FK506 and L. acidophilus, alone and in combination, on murine T-cell differentiation in vitro. Splenocytes from normal B6 mice were cultured with an anti-CD3 antibody in the presence of L. acidophilus (100 µg/mL) and/or FK506 (0.3 nM) for 72 h, after which the proportions of IFN-γ-producing T cells, IL-17-producing T cells, and Treg cells were assessed by flow cytometry, and cytokine concentrations were measured. The effects on CD8+ type 1 cytotoxic T (Tc1) and IL-17-producing CD8+ (Tc17) cells were also determined. Alloreactive T-cell proliferation was evaluated in a mixed lymphocyte reaction, and the suppressive capacity of combination-induced Treg cells was assessed in co-culture. Finally, RNA-seq was performed on CD4+ T cells treated with L. acidophilus and/or FK506 to characterize gene expression changes associated with combination treatment.

The combination of FK506 and L. acidophilus significantly decreased Th1 and Th17 cell proliferation and significantly increased the proportion of Treg cells compared with vehicle and either single treatment. IFN-γ and IL-17 concentrations were decreased, whereas IL-10 was significantly increased in combination-treated cells. The combination treatment also significantly suppressed Tc1 and Tc17 cell proliferation, with L. acidophilus alone being more effective than FK506 alone on Tc1 cells. In the mixed lymphocyte reaction, the combination treatment significantly suppressed alloreactive T-cell proliferation compared with alloantigen stimulation alone or single treatments. Treg cells induced by the combination treatment significantly suppressed alloreactive T-cell proliferation in co-culture. RNA-seq analysis identified 6507 differentially expressed genes between anti-CD3-treated and combination-treated CD4+ T cells, including downregulation of Th1-related genes (Tbx21, Lgals7) and Th17-related genes (Il13, Mapk13, Il21, Slc26a10, Gzma) and upregulation of Treg-related genes (Nt5e, Foxp3, Ikzf2, Nrp1, Itgb8) in combination-treated cells.

The combination of FK506 and L. acidophilus exerts a synergistic immunoregulatory effect by modulating the Th17/Treg balance and suppressing alloreactive T-cell responses, supporting its potential as a therapeutic strategy for aGVHD.
