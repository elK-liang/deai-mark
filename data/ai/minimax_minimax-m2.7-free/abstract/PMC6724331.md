Abstract  
Circular RNAs (circRNAs) are abundant, stable non‑coding transcripts with emerging roles in cancer, yet the full repertoire of circRNAs in human lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) and squamous cell carcinoma (LUSC) remains poorly defined.  
We enriched circRNAs by RNase R digestion of ribosomal‑RNA‑depleted total RNA from 10 paired non‑small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) tumor and matching normal tissue samples. High‑throughput RNA‑seq libraries were analyzed with five computational pipelines (CIRCexplorer2, circRNA_finder, CIRI2, find_circ, MapSplice) to identify back‑spliced junctions. Only circRNAs called by all five programs were retained to ensure high confidence.  
We identified 17 952 circRNAs, 98.8 % of which were supported by ≥2 unique back‑spliced reads. The circRNAs were distributed across all chromosomes and predominantly exonic (77.4 %), with most containing 2–5 exons and ranging from 200 to 1 200 nucleotides in length (91.7 %). Alternative back‑splicing was prevalent: 60.8 % of host genes produced at least two distinct circRNA isoforms, and some genes generated more than ten circRNAs. Cross‑referencing with circBase (140 790 human circRNAs) revealed 2 593 novel circRNAs not previously documented. Differential expression analysis comparing tumor and normal tissues identified 50 circRNAs significantly altered in LUAD (adjusted p ≤ 0.05, |log₂FC| ≥ 1). Sanger sequencing and qPCR validated the up‑regulation of hsa_circ_0002360 in LUAD samples.  
This comprehensive atlas of lung cancer circRNAs uncovers extensive alternative back‑splicing and identifies numerous novel tumor‑associated circRNAs, providing a valuable resource for future functional studies in NSCLC.
