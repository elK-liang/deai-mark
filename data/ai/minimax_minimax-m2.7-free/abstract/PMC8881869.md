Abstract

Background  
Acute graft‑versus‑host disease (aGVHD) is driven by donor T‑cell activation, especially Th1 and Th17 responses, and by a deficit of regulatory T cells (Tregs). We evaluated the immunomodulatory potential of combined FK506 (a calcineurin inhibitor) and Lactobacillus acidophilus (a probiotic) on murine T‑cell differentiation and gene expression in vitro.

Methods  
Splenocytes from normal C57BL/6 mice were stimulated with anti‑CD3 for 72 h in the presence of L. acidophilus (100 µg/mL), FK506 (0.3 nM), or the two agents together. Th1, Th17, and Treg proportions were assessed by intracellular staining; cytokine levels (IFN‑γ, IL‑17, IL‑10) were measured by ELISA. CD8⁺ Tc1 and Tc17 cells were similarly analyzed. Alloreactive T‑cell proliferation was examined in a mixed‑lymphocyte reaction (MLR), and sorted combination‑induced Tregs were co‑cultured with allogeneic responders. Transcriptomic changes in CD4⁺ T cells were profiled by RNA‑seq (≥2‑fold differential expression cut‑off).

Results  
The combination treatment markedly reduced Th1 (IFN‑γ⁺) and Th17 (IL‑17⁺) proliferation while increasing the frequency of Tregs compared with vehicle or either agent alone (p < 0.05). IFN‑γ and IL‑17 concentrations decreased, and IL‑10 rose in combination cultures. CD8⁺ Tc1 and Tc17 cells were also suppressed, with L. acidophilus alone being more effective than FK506 for Tc1. In MLRs, the combination suppressed alloreactive proliferation more robustly than single treatments, and co‑cultured induced Tregs retained suppressive activity. RNA‑seq identified 6 507 differentially expressed genes; the combination down‑regulated Th1‑related genes (Tbx21, Lgals7) and Th17‑related genes (Il13, Mapk13, Il21, Slc26a10, Gzma) while up‑regulating Treg‑associated genes (Nt5e, Foxp3, Ikzf2, Nrp1, Itgb8) relative to single treatments.
