Bladder carcinoma (BC) is a common malignancy with limited prognostic biomarkers; circular RNAs (circRNAs) have emerged as important regulators of gene expression in cancer. In this study we examined the circRNA expression profile in BC tissues by microarray analysis of three paired tumor‑normal samples and identified circRNA TFRC (cTFRC) as a consistently up‑regulated transcript in tumors. Validation by quantitative RT‑PCR in 57 paired BC specimens confirmed significantly higher cTFRC levels in tumor compared with adjacent normal tissue. Further analysis of a cohort of 220 BC patients revealed that cTFRC expression was elevated in recurrent tumors relative to primary lesions, and that increased cTFRC correlated with higher histological grade, advanced T stage, and presence of lymphatic metastasis. Kaplan‑Meier survival analysis showed that patients with high cTFRC expression had significantly poorer overall survival, and the associations between cTFRC levels and clinicopathological characteristics are summarized in Table 1. Together, these findings demonstrate that up‑regulation of cTFRC is associated with aggressive tumor behavior and adverse prognosis in BC, suggesting cTFRC as a potential prognostic biomarker and a candidate regulator that may function as a sponge for microRNA‑107 in bladder carcinoma progression.
