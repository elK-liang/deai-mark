Abstract  

From July 1 2014 to July 1 2019, 17 patients (21 episodes of complete response [CR] or near‑CR [nCR]) were retrospectively analyzed. The median age at CR/nCR was 16 years (range 4–39), 70.6 % were male, and 23.8 % had primary extensive lung/pleural metastases (L/Pmeta), 19.0 % developed L/Pmeta within 12 months of initial Ewing sarcoma (ES) diagnosis, and 57.1 % after >12 months. The median time from extensive L/Pmeta to first CR/nCR was 5.2 months; median follow‑up of surviving patients was 39.6 months (range 14.5–60.9). Treatments comprised VDC/IE (5 episodes), VIT (3), and AVI (13); 82.4 % of patients received whole‑lung irradiation and 16 episodes included maintenance therapy (chemotherapy, anti‑angiogenic tyrosine‑kinase inhibitor, PARP‑inhibitor, or NSAIDs). At last assessment, 3 patients were disease‑free, 6 were alive with disease, and 8 had died of disease (4 from lung/pleural recurrence, 4 from other distant metastases). Overall median event‑free survival (EFS) was 9.3 months (95 % CI 2.0–16.6) and overall survival (OS) 37.5 months (95 % CI 21.8–53.1); 2‑year EFS and OS were 19 % and 70.6 %, respectively. Among the 12 relapsed patients (all enrolled in trial NCT03416517), median EFS was 6.5 months (95 % CI 5.8–7.2) and OS 37.5 months (95 % CI 26.4–48.6); 2‑year EFS and OS were 16.7 % and 66.7 %. Lung/pleural relapse occurred in 71.4 % (15/21) of CR/nCR episodes. Notably, in 9 of these 15 recurrent cases the recurrent lesions were exactly in situ (coincident with the original metastatic sites); 4 of those also had additional distant metastases, while 5 remained confined to the lung/pleura. In 2 further cases, some lesions recurred in situ while others appeared elsewhere. Despite achieving CR/nCR, long‑term survival remains poor, with a high proportion of in‑situ lung/pleural recurrences, underscoring the need for improved local and systemic strategies.
