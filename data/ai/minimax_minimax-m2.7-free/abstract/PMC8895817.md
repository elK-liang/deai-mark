Targeted panel sequencing of the LPL, FNDC5 and PPARγ genes was performed in 77 healthy adolescents from Southern Italy to catalog genetic variability and explore its potential relationship with body composition and lipid metabolic profile. Sequencing produced a total of 1964 variants across the panel, of which 208 fell within the three genes of interest; after applying quality filters (FAO, FDP, GQ, QUAL) 111 high‑confidence polymorphisms remained (61 in LPL, 38 in FNDC5, 12 in PPARγ). These comprised 33 insertions/deletions, 77 single‑nucleotide substitutions and 1 other substitution, with the most frequent nucleotide changes being C/T and G/A (≈16.6 % and 14.1 %, respectively). Twenty‑four variants had not been reported previously (11 in LPL, 12 in FNDC5, 1 in PPARγ); five of these novel alleles had a minor allele frequency (MAF) ≥ 0.05, two were in the 0.01–0.05 range and seventeen were < 0.01. Functional annotation revealed that 40 variants were intronic, 53 were located in flanking regions (42 in the 3′UTR, 4 in the 5′UTR, 4 upstream, 1 intergenic, 2 regulatory), and 18 were exonic. The exonic variants included four frameshift, one stop‑codon (LPL p.S474X) and 14 single‑nucleotide changes, among which three non‑synonymous substitutions were identified in PPARγ (p.P12A, p.G290W, p.K156E), two frameshifts (p.L178Tfs2, p.D7Rfs5), three FNDC5 alterations (p.L132Ffs6, p.L81V, p.H127N), and nine LPL changes (four synonymous, four non‑synonymous – p.N318S, p.R197H, p.H348P, p.E36N – plus the stop‑codon and a frameshift p.Q118Hfs30). Using Haploview (R² ≥ 0.8), 52 tagSNPs were selected (34 in LPL, 19 in FNDC5, 3 in PPARγ); unlike the LPL locus, FNDC5 and PPARγ showed no detectable linkage disequilibrium among common SNPs. These findings provide a comprehensive catalog of coding and non‑coding variation in LPL, FNDC5 and PPARγ specific to an adolescent cohort from Southern Italy and identify a set of tagSNPs for future association studies linking these polymorphisms to anthropometric and lipid‑related traits.
