Abstract

Background  
Implantable venous access ports (IVAPs) are commonly used for chemotherapy delivery in breast cancer patients. This randomized controlled trial compared patient‑reported comfort and early complications between ultrasound‑guided internal jugular vein (IJV) and axillary/subclavian vein (AxV/SCV) puncture for IVAP placement.

Methods  
A total of 248 breast cancer patients requiring IVAP were randomized 1:1 to either IJV (n = 124) or AxV/SCV (n = 124) puncture. Procedure duration, patient comfort graded from 0 (no discomfort) to 5 (severe discomfort) on postoperative days 1, 2 and 7, and early complications (within 7 days) were recorded. Baseline characteristics were comparable between groups; three patients in the IJV group and one in the AxV/SCV group were lost to follow‑up.

Results  
Comfort scores were significantly better in the AxV/SCV group on day 1 (p < 0.001), day 2 (p < 0.001) and day 7 (p = 0.023). On day 1, most IJV patients had grade 1 (29.8 %) or grade 2 (43.5 %) versus grade 0 (66.9 %) or grade 1 (24.2 %) in the AxV/SCV group. Procedure time was shorter in the AxV/SCV group (27.14 ± 3.29 min vs 28.92 ± 2.54 min, p < 0.001). Early complications occurred in 11 patients (8.87 %) in the AxV/SCV group and 2 (1.61 %) in the IJV group (p = 0.019). The higher complication rate was driven by catheter misplacement (4.84 % vs 0 %, p = 0.029); misplacements were located in the right IJV (2), right subclavian vein (2) and left brachiocephalic vein (2). No pneumothorax, hemothorax or grade 5 events were observed; artery puncture and subcutaneous hematoma rates did not differ significantly between groups.

Conclusions  
AxV/SCV puncture provides superior patient comfort and a modestly shorter procedure time compared with IJV puncture, but it carries a higher risk of early complications, particularly catheter misplacement. These findings should guide site selection and may prompt additional technical safeguards when using the axillary/subclavian approach for IVAP placement in breast cancer patients.
