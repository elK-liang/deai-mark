Abstract  
Circular RNAs (circRNAs) are emerging as important regulators in cancer, but their landscape in gastric cancer (GC) remains incompletely characterized. To identify novel GC‑associated circRNAs, we interrogated circRNA microarray datasets (GSE83521 and GSE89143) from the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO).  
Differentially expressed circRNAs were intersected between the two datasets, yielding eight transcripts significantly downregulated in GC tissues. Based on expression level and detection specificity, we focused on hsa_circ_0061137 (circDIDO1). The back‑splicing junction was validated by PCR with divergent primers (cDNA vs. gDNA) and Sanger sequencing. RNase R resistance assay distinguished circDIDO1 from linear DIDO1 transcripts. Subcellular localization was assessed by RNA‑FISH. Quantitative RT‑PCR evaluated circDIDO1 expression in a panel of GC cell lines, normal gastric epithelial cells, and 102 paired tumor–adjacent normal tissues. Correlation between circDIDO1 levels and clinicopathological parameters, as well as patient survival, was analyzed.  
CircDIDO1 is generated by back‑splicing of DIDO1 exons 2–6, spans 1787 nucleotides, and is resistant to RNase R digestion, confirming its circular nature. It localizes to both the nucleus and cytoplasm. Expression of circDIDO1 was markedly lower in GC cell lines and tumor tissues compared with corresponding normal controls. Low circDIDO1 levels were significantly associated with larger tumor size, presence of distant metastasis, and shorter overall survival of GC patients.  
CircDIDO1 is a newly identified circular RNA that is downregulated in gastric cancer. Its reduced expression correlates with aggressive tumor features and poorer prognosis, suggesting that circDIDO1 may serve as a potential biomarker for GC progression. Further functional studies are warranted to elucidate its mechanistic role in gastric tumorigenesis.
