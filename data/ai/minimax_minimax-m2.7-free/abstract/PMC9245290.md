Circular RNAs (circRNAs) are increasingly recognized as important regulators of colorectal cancer (CRC) pathogenesis, yet the full repertoire of dysregulated circRNAs and their functional contributions remain incompletely understood. To profile circRNA expression in CRC, we performed ribosomal RNA‑depleted total RNA sequencing on six paired tumor and adjacent normal tissue samples. Bioinformatics analysis identified 11,141 circRNAs, of which approximately 90 % (10,064) were derived from exon regions, and revealed 71 significantly dysregulated circRNAs (32 up‑regulated and 39 down‑regulated) based on a log2 fold‑change > 1 or < − 1 and P < 0.05. Hierarchical clustering, scatter and volcano plots illustrated the distinct expression patterns of these circRNAs across the CRC cohort. Among the up‑regulated candidates, hsa_circ_0006357, designated circEZH2, caught our attention; it is generated by head‑to‑tail splicing of EZH2 exons 2 and 3 (253 bp) and was experimentally validated by PCR amplification and Sanger sequencing. Expression analysis in a panel of human CRC cell lines (COLO205, HCT15, LoVo, SW620, HT‑29 and HCT116) confirmed detectable levels of circEZH2. The genome‑wide distribution and chromosomal localization of the identified circRNAs were further visualized with Circos plots, underscoring the widespread presence of exon‑derived circRNAs in CRC. Together, these results provide a comprehensive circRNA landscape of CRC and highlight circEZH2 as a novel, abundantly expressed circular RNA that is up‑regulated in tumor tissues, suggesting a potential role in CRC progression and warranting further investigation into its interactions with miR‑133b, IGF2BP2 and CREB1.
