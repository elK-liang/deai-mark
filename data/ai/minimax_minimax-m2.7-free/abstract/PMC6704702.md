Abstract  
Gastric cancer (GC) metastasis is a leading cause of mortality, yet the repertoire of metastasis‑associated circular RNAs (circRNAs) remains incompletely characterized.  
RNA‑seq was performed on 3 GC tissues with distant metastasis and 2 GC tissues without metastasis. Differential expression analysis identified circRNAs with a fold change > 2.0 and p < 0.05. Candidate circRNAs were validated by qRT‑PCR, Sanger sequencing of the back‑splice junction, RNase R resistance assay, and nuclear‑cytoplasmic fractionation. Expression levels were further examined in 93 paired fresh‑frozen GC and matched normal tissue samples.  
Among 38 differentially expressed circRNAs, circNHSL1 (hsa_circ_0006835), a 335‑nt transcript spliced from the NHSL1 gene (chr6:138,743,180–138,893,726), showed the strongest up‑regulation in metastatic tumors. Sanger sequencing confirmed the head‑to‑tail splicing event. circNHSL1 resisted RNase R digestion and was predominantly localized in the cytoplasm, indicating high stability. Its expression was markedly higher in several GC cell lines compared with the normal gastric epithelial cell line GES‑1 and was significantly elevated in tumor tissues relative to matched normal tissues, especially in cases with metastasis.  
circNHSL1 is a stable, cytoplasm‑enriched circRNA that is overexpressed in metastatic gastric cancer. Its differential expression between metastatic and non‑metastatic tissues suggests potential value as a diagnostic or prognostic biomarker. Functional experiments demonstrate that circNHSL1 promotes gastric cancer progression through the miR‑1306‑3p/SIX1/vimentin axis.
