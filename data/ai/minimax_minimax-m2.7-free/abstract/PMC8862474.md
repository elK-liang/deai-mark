Abstract

Chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) is characterized by profound immune dysregulation, yet the intrinsic mechanisms by which tumor‑derived factors alter T‑cell behavior remain incompletely understood. We hypothesized that the anti‑apoptotic protein BCL‑2, frequently overexpressed in CLL cells, may also be expressed in T cells and contribute to immunosuppression.

Peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) from 266 CLL patients and age‑matched healthy controls were analyzed by flow cytometry for BCL‑2 expression (positive defined as >20 % of cells) and mean fluorescence intensity (MFI) in total T cells and in CD4⁺ and CD8⁺ subsets (n = 110). Clinical correlations were assessed using χ² tests. For mechanistic insight, PBMCs from six newly diagnosed CLL patients were subjected to single‑cell RNA‑sequencing (scRNA‑seq). BCL‑2‑positive and BCL‑2‑negative T cells were compared using the limma package for differential expression, followed by KEGG pathway and gene set enrichment analyses (GSEA).

BCL‑2 protein levels were significantly elevated in total T cells of CLL patients compared with controls (MFI and percent positive cells, P < 0.05). Both CD4⁺ and CD8⁺ T cells from CLL patients showed higher BCL‑2 expression and MFI than those from healthy donors. BCL‑2 expression in T cells correlated positively with serum β2‑microglobulin (χ² = 3.916, P < 0.05). scRNA‑seq analysis identified a distinct cluster of CD4⁺ T cells with high BCL‑2 (Cluster 6). Differential gene expression between BCL‑2‑positive and BCL‑2‑negative T cells revealed enrichment of genes involved in cell‑cell adhesion and leukocyte adhesion pathways, as well as T‑cell activation processes. GSEA demonstrated that BCL‑2‑positive T cells were enriched for regulatory T‑cell (Treg) signatures and showed a marked reduction in the Th1‑type cytokine signature, indicating a shift toward an immunosuppressive and functionally impaired phenotype.

BCL‑2 is aberrantly overexpressed in T cells from CLL patients and is associated with clinical markers of disease burden. Single‑cell profiling indicates that BCL‑2 expression promotes a Treg‑biased differentiation program and suppresses Th1‑type responses, suggesting that BCL‑2 contributes to immune evasion in CLL by fostering an immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment.
