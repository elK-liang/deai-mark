Colorectal cancer (CRC) is a leading cause of cancer‑related mortality worldwide, and emerging evidence indicates that circular RNAs (circRNAs) play critical roles in tumor pathogenesis. In this study we performed high‑throughput sequencing (PMID25624062) and identified circEXOC6B (hsa_circ_0009043), a 390‑bp circRNA generated from exons 3–6 of EXOC6B, as the second most down‑regulated circRNA in colon cancer tissues; this down‑regulation was also confirmed in the independent GEO dataset GSE77661. Sanger sequencing of the back‑splicing junction, amplification with divergent primers, and resistance to RNase R and actinomycin D confirmed that circEXOC6B is a genuine circRNA with a closed‑loop structure and greater stability than linear GAPDH mRNA. Quantitative RT‑PCR analysis of 78 paired CRC samples and an independent cDNA microarray (80 cancer vs. 15 adjacent tissues) revealed significantly reduced circEXOC6B expression in tumor tissue, and low expression correlated with larger tumor size, presence of lymphatic metastasis and higher TNM stage. Functional investigations demonstrated that circEXOC6B binds to RRAGB, an activator of mTORC1, and antagonizes the HIF1A‑RRAGB‑mTORC1 positive feedback loop, thereby inhibiting CRC progression. These findings establish circEXOC6B as a tumor‑suppressive circRNA that restrains colorectal cancer growth by disrupting the HIF1A‑RRAGB‑mTORC1 signaling axis and suggest its potential as a diagnostic biomarker and therapeutic target for CRC.
