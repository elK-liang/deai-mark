Abstract: Determining which gene variants are causal for a disease requires systematic application of evidence criteria combined with expert review. We present the development of the ClinGen Variant Curation Interface (VCI), a genetic variant biocuration platform for health care providers, researchers and the medical genetics community. The VCI supports the FDA-recognized ClinGen variant curation process and combines clinical, genetic, population and functional evidence with expert review to classify variants into the ACMG/AMP 2015 variant classification guideline categories. Primary features of the VCI include the ability to curate individually or in groups, associate pertinent evidence with variant classifications, assess evidence per variant curation disease/gene-specific protocols, save provisional records, support an expert review process of curated evidence, and automatically publish classifications and underlying criteria assessments. To ensure compliance with FDA requirements for the auditability of the ClinGen variant curation process, the VCI database maintains a complete audit trail of all saved curation actions. The current VCI v2.0 platform has over 6300 variant classifications in different curation stages, and its modernized architecture is able to scale to support over 1 million future classifications. Future improvements focus on enhancing scale, workflow and throughput, including task management with assigned variant classification records and action items, support for bulk variant curation workflows, automatic ingestion of additional variant evidence with monitoring of major data changes via a streaming service, and customized curation experiences based on Variant Curation Expert Panel specifications. The VCI thus provides an auditable, scalable infrastructure for variant classification according to ACMG/AMP guidelines.
