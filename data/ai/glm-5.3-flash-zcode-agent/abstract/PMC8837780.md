Long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs) contribute to the progression of epithelial ovarian cancer (EOC), and their systematic identification may reveal new tumor suppressors and biomarkers. By analyzing EOC-related lncRNA expression profiles in the Gene Expression Omnibus datasets GSE135886 and GSE119054, we identified 26 downregulated and 4 upregulated lncRNAs in EOC tissues. Overlapping the top 10 downregulated lncRNAs of the two datasets yielded five common candidates (SNHG10, TRHDE-AS1, linc00476, linc00893 and NR2F2-AS1), and quantitative real-time PCR showed that all five were expressed at low levels, with SNHG10 displaying the lowest expression. Analysis of The Cancer Genome Atlas further demonstrated significant downregulation of SNHG10 in EOC (P < 0.001). Kaplan-Meier analysis showed that low SNHG10 expression was correlated with shorter progression-free survival (P = 0.001) and overall survival (P = 0.019). Cox proportional hazard regression identified intraperitoneal metastasis and SNHG10 expression as significantly correlated with progression-free survival, and lymph node metastasis, intraperitoneal metastasis and SNHG10 expression as significantly correlated with overall survival. Mechanistically, SNHG10 upregulates BIN1 to suppress the tumorigenesis and epithelial-mesenchymal transition of EOC by sponging miR-200a-3p. These results indicate that low SNHG10 expression is significantly correlated with the prognosis of EOC, and that SNHG10 may be regarded as a potential independent predictive biomarker and tumor-suppressive lncRNA in EOC patients.
