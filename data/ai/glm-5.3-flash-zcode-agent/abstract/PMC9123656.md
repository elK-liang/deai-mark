Abstract: Camrelizumab is a humanized anti-PD-1 antibody, and we evaluated its safety, activity and pharmacokinetics in advanced Asian patients with melanoma. From April 2016 to October 2017, 36 patients with advanced melanoma who had failed previous first-line treatment were enrolled. In the first stage, nine patients (three in each of three dose groups) were included in the dose-limiting toxicity (DLT) analysis set; in the second stage, doses were expanded to nine patients per group, with 12 patients in each dose group receiving at least one dose and 33 patients receiving at least one efficacy evaluation. The median age was 52 years (range 29-68), with 17 male and 19 female patients. Primary sites included acral (50.0%), mucosal (27.8%) and cutaneous and unknown origins (19.4%), and the most common metastatic sites were lymph nodes (77.8%), lung (58.3%) and liver (33.3%); more than half of the patients (66.7%) had received at least two previous lines of chemotherapy or targeted therapy. All 36 patients received at least one dose of camrelizumab, and no DLT was observed in the three dose groups, with the maximum tolerated dose not reached. Treatment-related adverse events occurred in 29 cases (80.6%), most commonly reactive cutaneous capillary endothelial proliferation (63.9%) and hypothyroidism (25.0%); grade 3 or above events comprised one case of liver injury that recovered after symptomatic treatment and two cases of elevated γ-glutamyltransferase and blood triglycerides without clinical symptoms, and no treatment-related deaths occurred. These results establish the safety, pharmacokinetics and preliminary activity of camrelizumab in advanced Asian melanoma patients.
