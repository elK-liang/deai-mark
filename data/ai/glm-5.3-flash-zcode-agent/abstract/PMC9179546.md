Lower-grade gliomas (LGGs) are classified by IDH status and 1p/19q co-deletion according to the WHO 2021 stratification, and their subtype structure can be further resolved by integrating transcriptome, DNA methylome and copy number data. We generated integrated multi-omics maps of LGGs, classifying cases into four genetic groups: IDH-wildtype (IDH-wt) gliomas; IDH-mutant oligodendrogliomas (IDH-O) with co-deletion of chromosomes 1p and 19q; and IDH-mutant astrocytomas without co-deletion (IDH-A), where IDH-A' in contrast to IDH-A carries a chromosome 19q deletion. Combined gains of chromosome 7 and losses of chromosome 10, characteristic of WHO grade IV glioblastomas, accumulated in IDH-wt LGG (12 of 19 cases, 63%), whereas in IDH-mutant LGG these alterations appeared mostly uncombined. IDH-O LGG were characterized by a high amount of TERT promoter methylation, and mean methylation values averaged over the tumors of each group increased in the order IDH-wt < IDH-A/A' < IDH-O, with the G-CIMP signature characterizing the hypermethylation of IDH-mutant LGG. G-CIMP LGG had better prognosis than IDH-wt in terms of the hazard ratio, and the prognosis of IDH-O was slightly better than that of IDH-A/A'. Expression subtypes E1-E8 and methylation subtypes M1-M6 accumulated to different degrees in the genetic groups, namely E1 and M1 in IDH-wt, E6 and M5 in IDH-O, and E2-E4 and M2-M4 in IDH-A/A', while the neuronal-type E7, E8 and M6 subtypes reflected samples of reduced tumor cell content. These integrated multi-omics maps link genetic, epigenomic and transcriptomic modes of genomic regulation in lower-grade gliomas.
