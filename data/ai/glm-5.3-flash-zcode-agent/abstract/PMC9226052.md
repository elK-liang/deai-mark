Abstract: The architecture and kinematics of regional normal faults strongly influence their seismic behavior, and fault systems crossing major ridges are among the least resolved. We integrated approximately 370 field structural data, comprising fault and slip attitudes with corresponding fault traces, together with fault traces from the literature, to produce a detailed structural map of the regional-scale CMF. The individual slickensides were grouped into fourteen composite sites lying on continuous and homogeneous portions of the fault, and for each site we calculated the average fault plane, the average slip vector, the rake and the characteristics of the maximum tension axis, which are represented as pseudo-focal mechanisms. The slickensides, which are fairly evenly distributed along strike, depict a fault with normal dip-slip kinematics that assumes a progressively higher dextral trans-tensive component toward the southeast. Analysis of the T-axes of deformation, showing substantial consistency with geodetic data, highlights a kinematic partitioning that is strongly influenced by the segmentation of the fault. The fault strike is ~N120° between the municipalities of Caggiano and Montemurro, deviating from the ~N160° trend of the Vallo di Diano basin to follow a trend sub-parallel to the Val d'Agri basin. Field mapping delineated a highly segmented but substantially continuous tectonic structure connecting the SSW-dipping border fault of the Auletta basin with the eastern Val d'Agri fault system, crossing the Monti della Maddalena ridge, with a well-defined right en-échelon geometric pattern. These results reveal the complex architecture of trans-ridge normal faults and their role in controlling large earthquakes.
