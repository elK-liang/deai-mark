Abstract  
Advanced melanoma in Asian patients often has limited treatment options after failure of first‑line therapy, highlighting the need for novel immunotherapeutic agents such as the anti‑PD‑1 antibody camrelizumab. This phase I study evaluated the safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics, and preliminary activity of camrelizumab in this population.  
Patients with advanced melanoma who had progressed after at least one prior line of treatment were enrolled between April 2016 and October 2017. The study used a standard 3 + 3 dose‑escalation design with three dose levels of camrelizumab administered intravenously. Primary endpoints included dose‑limiting toxicities (DLTs), determination of the maximum tolerated dose (MTD), and characterization of pharmacokinetics. Secondary endpoints assessed preliminary antitumor activity and adverse events.  
Thirty‑six patients received at least one dose of camrelizumab (12 per dose group). No DLTs were observed in any cohort, and the MTD was not reached. Treatment‑related adverse events occurred in 29 patients (80.6%), most commonly reactive cutaneous capillary endothelial proliferation (RCCEP; 63.9%), hypothyroidism (25.0%), rash (13.9%), fever (11.1%), hyperthyroidism (8.3%), and vitiligo or fatigue (5.6% each). Grade 3 or higher events were limited to one case of liver injury and two instances of elevated γ‑glutamyltransferase/triglycerides, all resolving with appropriate management. No adverse events led to treatment interruption or death. Thirty‑three patients completed at least two dosing cycles; seven patients remained on therapy for more than one year, and four continued beyond two years, indicating durability of response in a subset of participants.  
Camrelizumab demonstrated a manageable safety profile with no dose‑limiting toxicities in Asian patients with advanced melanoma. The drug was well tolerated at the doses evaluated, with RCCEP being the most frequent low‑grade event. Preliminary clinical activity was evident, with several patients achieving prolonged treatment exposure. These results support further investigation of camrelizumab in melanoma and other tumor types.
